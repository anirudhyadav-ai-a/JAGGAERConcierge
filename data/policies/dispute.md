---
id: POL-DISPUTE-01
title: Card transaction disputes
---
Customers may dispute a card transaction within 60 days of the statement date on which it first appeared. A dispute can be opened in the app, by phone or in a branch, and the customer should describe why the charge is wrong.

Provisional credit is usually issued within 10 business days while the claim is reviewed. If the review finds the charge was valid, the provisional credit is reversed and the customer is told why.

Disputes cover unauthorised charges, duplicate charges, goods that never arrived and amounts that differ from the receipt. A refund agreed directly with the merchant closes the dispute.
