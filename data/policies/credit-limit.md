id: POL-LIMIT-04
title: Credit limit increase
---
Credit limit increase requests are reviewed after 6 months of account history and may involve a soft credit inquiry. Requests can be made once every 6 months.

The review looks at income, on-time payment history and how much of the current limit is in use. A soft inquiry does not affect the customer's credit score.

If a request is declined, the customer receives the main reasons and may ask again after 6 months.
