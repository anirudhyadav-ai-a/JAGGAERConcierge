title: Foreign transaction fees
---
A 3% foreign transaction fee applies to purchases made in a foreign currency, except on cards that list no foreign transaction fees. The fee is charged on the converted amount and shown as a separate line on the statement.

Purchases made abroad but charged in US dollars may still carry the fee if the merchant processes them outside the United States. Cash withdrawals abroad follow the ATM fee schedule instead.

Customers who travel often can ask about cards with no foreign transaction fees.
