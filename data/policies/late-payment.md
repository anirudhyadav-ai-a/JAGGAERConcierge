id: POL-LATE-03
title: Late payment
---
A $35 late fee applies if the minimum payment is not received by the due date. Customers may request a one-time courtesy waiver of the late payment fee per 12 months.

A payment counts as on time when it is received by 5 pm Eastern on the due date. Payments made through the app or by autopay are credited the same day.

Two or more late payments in a row may lead to a higher interest rate and are reported to the credit bureaus after 30 days.
